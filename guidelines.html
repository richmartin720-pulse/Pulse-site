Pulse Community Guidelines

Pulse is a global platform for sharing perspectives through short video responses. We encourage respectful discussion and constructive debate.

Content not allowed

  • Harassment, bullying, or threats
  • Hate speech or discrimination
  • Violence or promotion of illegal activity
  • Explicit sexual content
  • Spam or misleading content

Moderation

User content on Pulse may be automatically reviewed using moderation technology and may also be reviewed manually if reported by other users.

Reporting content

Users can report content directly in the app. Reported content may be reviewed and removed if it violates these guidelines.

Account actions

Accounts that repeatedly violate the guidelines may be restricted or removed.

Contact

If you have concerns about content on Pulse, please contact:

support@pulse-app.global
